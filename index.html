ONE THOUGHTFUL LEARNING LOOP
  1. 01

    Start with a story.

    42 beads, a gathering, and 17 beads to share. A concrete reason to work out what remains.

  2. 02

    Make room to explore.

    Warm-ups, visual groups of ten, helpful hints, and an optional next challenge.

  3. 03

    Reflect, then return.

    Separate feedback on accuracy, hints, and active time. Optional learner feedback. A saved place to come back to.
